How fast is it on this box?

Model

To change the model, sign in to the assistant with the owner account first, then come back to this page.

    Good to know

    • Private. Your chats and documents stay on this box. Nothing is sent to an AI company. The box only goes online to download models and updates.
    • It runs on the processor. It does not use a graphics chip (no AVADO box can use one for AI yet), so answers come at a steady pace, not instantly. When you add a document to a chat, the assistant reads all of it first, so a long document can take a few minutes before the answer starts.
    • Your node comes first. While the assistant is answering, your other apps get priority, and the model is unloaded from memory after 10 minutes without use. The first answer after a pause takes longer while the model loads again.
    • Bigger is smarter, but needs more. Big models need a lot of memory and free space. The page suggests the biggest one that fits next to your node.
    • It can be wrong. Check important answers, especially about money, health or law.
